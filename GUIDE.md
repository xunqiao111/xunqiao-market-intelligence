# Xun Qiao practical user guide

**[Open the website](https://xunqiao.qq10.fun/) · [Full feature introduction](README.md) · [Developer API guide](API-GUIDE.md)**

## Your first visit

Start on the public homepage. You can inspect market focus, asset overviews, related events, recent news and upcoming dates before registering. On a slow connection the first visit can take longer while the interface downloads; watch the progress message rather than repeatedly refreshing.

Create a free account, complete the email verification and sign in to use the research pages. Ordinary membership is free. Gold is optional and adds GPT replies and developer API calls; it is not required to read published AI Picks and research reports.

## A short research session

### 1. Pick the instrument carefully

Open Workbench and start with BTC or NVDA, or use Add asset. Search by ticker or name and select the correct category. Read the full instrument name and market: the same ticker letters can refer to a crypto asset, an ETF or a tokenized instrument.

You can select several assets but investigate one active focus at a time. Changing the focus changes its chart, related news and research context. The asset selector's confirmation control applies the chosen selection; do not confuse it with placing a trade.

### 2. Establish the price context

Choose 5m, 1h or 1D as appropriate. Read the available OHLC values, market status and terminal timing. Open event markers only when needed. A marker means that a report belongs at that time on the timeline; it is not proof of causation.

Compare the current quote with the displayed range, volume and capitalization. An equity session range and a 24-hour crypto range are different windows. A share count is not a dollar turnover value. Use the labels and units supplied by the page.

### 3. Read the underlying reporting

Review related events for the active asset. Open a story, inspect the available analysis and follow the original source. For an event set, distinguish the synthesized summary from the constituent reports.

Direct, indirect and watch links express different association strengths. Click tags to read their definitions. One story can have different implications for different assets, so do not apply a card's overall impression indiscriminately to every tagged instrument.

### 4. Challenge a report before using it

Open the AI report and read beyond its title and stars. Identify the observed window, proposed conditions, confirmation trigger, invalidation level and competing evidence.

Ask whether the data is sufficient, whether repeated coverage is being counted as independent evidence, and whether technical and news inputs agree. A subjective probability is not a verified win rate. The example account used for sizing is not your own account balance.

### 5. Leave a useful next check

Use notes or a research plan to preserve the question you want to revisit. A useful condition is specific enough to recognize later: a price revisiting a zone, a source publishing a confirmation, or a scheduled event resolving an uncertainty.

Plans are observation tools. They do not submit orders. Later, compare report versions and inspect the five-hour review when the window has ended and sufficient prices are available.

## Choose the right news view

| View | Use it when | Useful controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All news | You want the latest incoming material or a specific story. | Search, filters, sources, bookmarks. |
| Official | You want first-party announcements and company updates. | Source and keyword selection, event details. |
| AI Picks | You want a prioritized list and grouped developments. | Importance / time sorting, event sets, macro watch. |
| AI Reports | You want an asset-specific research scenario. | Asset filter, full report, rating breakdown, comparisons and review. |

Choose sources according to your reading needs. A source being enabled does not mean it publishes continuously. An empty filtered result and a feed that is still loading are different states.

## Reading event signals

**Spheres and rings** represent bullish and bearish interpretations for a linked asset. **Gold and red stars** express importance. **Orange** identifies macro observations. **Green and purple** events still participate in the relationship system.

Importance, direction, strength of association and confidence are not interchangeable. Do not use a star count as a shortcut around reading the evidence.

Card counts and underlying-record counts can differ. An event set can contain several distinct reports, and an asset can also be associated indirectly through a sector or macro relationship.

## Going deeper with observations

Open the research desk to inspect available derivatives, on-chain, stablecoin, DeFi, network, yield, unlock or asset-profile observations. Categories vary with the active instrument and actual source coverage.

Keep the source, timestamp, unit and status attached to a value. Do not average incompatible turnover definitions, annualize a rate whose period is unknown, or read a historical third-party report as a current on-chain measurement. Missing values are not zero.

## Using the markets page

Use market categories and ticker / name search to find an instrument. Sort by related events, change, price or ticker, and use the watchlist view to narrow your own list.

The chart-and-events view focuses on price and reporting time. The data-and-research view adds asset snapshots and source-grouped research leads. Continue into the workbench when you want the same instrument surrounded by a wider research context.

## Asking GPT useful questions

Gold members can attach assets and reporting context and ask follow-up questions. Useful starting questions include:

- Which facts are confirmed, and which interpretations remain tentative?
- What would invalidate the report's current scenario?
- Which observations use incompatible units or market windows?
- What changed between these two pieces of reporting?

Check citations and timestamps. No personal provider key is required. Do not send a password, verification code, activation code or API secret in a conversation.

## Preferences and alerts

Switch language and theme in the header. Settings provide separate dark and light background choices, colors, motion options and reading preferences. Choosing a static background or reduced motion offers a calmer experience without changing the research information.

Announcements become available after login. Market and research alerts are opt-in; set the assets, importance and batching conditions you actually want. A notification is a request to review information, not a trading instruction.

## Membership and activation

The personal center separates your membership card from settings. Check your current tier and validity before using premium features. Redeem an activation code on the intended account and review the confirmation before completing it.

Gold number-pattern selection is a one-time choice. The service assigns a unique number; it is a membership identifier rather than a financial account. Do not publish your card number in a screenshot.

## Connecting your own tools

Read the [developer API guide](API-GUIDE.md) for the 26 query recipes. Use stable instrument IDs, choose minimum read-only scopes and keep your key on your server. Private-workspace routes expose only your own authorized data. Real-time candles are excluded.

## Troubleshooting and feedback

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| First visit takes longer | Read the loading message; initial resources must download. |
| A filtered view is empty | Check search terms, category, source selection and whether loading has finished. |
| Two similarly named assets disagree | Check instrument ID, category, venue, currency and session. |
| A metric is missing | Coverage may be unavailable; do not infer a zero value. |
| A review is pending | The five-hour window or required price data may not be complete. |
| An API call is denied | Check membership, key validity, scopes, limits and source permissions. |

For a reproducible issue, include your page, device, browser, steps, expected result and a screenshot with personal information removed. Never post passwords, verification codes, activation codes, API keys, membership numbers or private conversations.
