# Xun Qiao user guide

[Back to the introduction](README.md) · [Open Xun Qiao](https://xunqiao.qq10.fun/)

## Start with the homepage

Guests can browse the complete homepage, including market focus, asset overviews, related events, headlines and upcoming dates. Register and verify your email for a free ordinary membership. The first visit downloads interface resources; watch the loading indicator while other pages prepare in the background.

## Choose research assets

The workbench starts with BTC and NVDA. Add assets, then select an active research focus. Check the full name, symbol, market and venue: identical ticker letters can represent different instruments. Use the homepage market filter to explore crypto and U.S. stocks.

## Read news your way

| View | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| All news | Browse incoming messages; search and filter a specific lead |
| Official updates | Read collected official announcements and company updates |
| AI Picks | Prioritize important events and follow grouped developments |
| AI Reports | Explore structured research, conditions, risks and reviews |

Choose sources, search by symbol or message, and bookmark items for later. Click tags for explanations. Read the analysis and original source before relying on a headline.

## Compare charts and evidence

Switch chart intervals and enable event markers only when needed. Markers locate reporting in time; they do not establish causation. Inspect related events, price ranges, volume and market cap for the active asset, then review derivatives, on-chain and other observations.

Equity volume counts shares. Crypto volume and turnover use their displayed currency units. Different sources can cover different venues and windows. Funding, open interest, DeFi TVL and protocol revenue are observations, not promises of tokenholder income or future returns.

## Follow up on a report

**Research plans** preserve conditions to observe. They do not place orders.

**Report comparisons** highlight how conclusions and conditions changed as new evidence arrived.

**Five-hour reviews** examine subsequent price paths once the window and inputs are complete. Missing prices and uncertain within-candle sequences remain uncertain. Hypothetical reviews are not realized trading performance.

## Notifications and appearance

Site announcements become available after login. Market and research alerts are opt-in. Manage your assets, importance and batching preferences in the site controls.

Use the header for theme and language switches. Settings offer separate dark and light backgrounds, colors and dynamic or static options. Desktop uses left navigation; mobile uses five bottom tabs.

## Premium features

Free members can view the API page. AI conversation replies and developer API key creation and calls require premium access. Bring specific asset and event context into your questions and verify the response against evidence.

Follow the [API documentation](https://xunqiao.qq10.fun/api-docs) for news, picks, reports, assets, related events, quote snapshots and research data. Real-time candles are excluded. Keep access keys private and outside public code.

## Feedback

Open an Issue with your device, browser, page, steps and expected result. Remove account details, private notes, passwords, verification codes and keys from screenshots. Current availability and entitlements are described on the website.
